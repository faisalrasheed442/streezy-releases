Streezy is a streaming app, so it does connect to the internet: to the places you stream to and the chats you add. This page lists every connection it makes, and nothing else is sent anywhere.

What Streezy is

Streezy is a desktop streaming app for Windows made by Faisal Malik at zylio. It runs on your computer and is built on the OBS Studio engine. zylio runs no server for it: there is no Streezy account, cloud or backend.

Every connection Streezy makes

  • Your streams. When you go live, Streezy sends your video and sound to the servers of the platforms you added (for example Twitch, YouTube or Kick), using the stream keys you gave it. Those platforms' own privacy policies apply to what you stream.
  • Chat you add. If you enter a channel name on the Chat page, Streezy reads that channel's public chat: Twitch chat (irc-ws.chat.twitch.tv), Kick chat (kick.com and its chat service ws-us2.pusher.com) and YouTube live chat (www.youtube.com). Reading needs no login. If you paste a Twitch chat token to send messages, it is used only with Twitch.
  • The internet check, when you run it. Streezy uploads random test data to Cloudflare's speed test (speed.cloudflare.com) for about ten seconds to measure your upload. It runs only when you press the button, in the setup or in Settings.
  • Checking for updates, once at start-up. Streezy asks GitHub (api.github.com/repos/faisalrasheed442/streezy-releases/releases/latest) whether a newer version exists. The request carries no cookies and no identifier; its User-Agent says only Streezy/<version>. GitHub sees your IP address, as it does for any visit, under GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing is downloaded until you click, and an update is checked against its published checksum before it runs. You can switch the check off in Settings › Privacy.
  • Web pages you add to a scene. A browser source or overlay loads whatever address you give it, like a web browser would. Streezy's built-in overlays load only from your own computer.

What Streezy opens on your own network

  • Overlays are served from your PC to your PC only (port 17887). Other devices cannot open them.
  • The phone remote can be opened by devices on your home network and needs your PIN. You can switch it off, or change the PIN, on the Remote page.
  • The remote port for Stream Deck and OBS tools (port 4455) needs its password. You can switch it off on the Remote page.

What Streezy never does

  • Send usage statistics, crash reports, analytics or any identifier to zylio or anyone else.
  • Upload your recordings, clips or scenes anywhere. They stay in your Videos folder.
  • Show ads, or share or sell any information with anyone.
  • Store your stream keys in a plain file. They go into Windows Credential Manager.

What stays on your computer

Streezy keeps its settings, scene collections, stream reports and logs in %APPDATA%\Streezy, and your recordings and clips in Videos\Streezy. Only you can see them. Logs never leave your computer unless you choose to send one to us yourself; the stream doctor's support report leaves out keys and passwords.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your message only to answer it. We never ask for your stream keys; if you share a log to show a problem, it is deleted once the problem is fixed.
